Example

The rules are changing

A useful example is hiring.

Anthropic candidate guidance
Anthropic says candidates can use Claude to refine applications and prepare for interviews, as long as the work is still honest and personal. They draw the line at live interviews and assessments unless AI is clearly allowed.
The question is not simply: did you use AI?
It is: did you use it in a sensible, honest way?
The gap

The workforce is ahead of the workplace

People are using AI. Their employers haven't caught up.

79%
use their own AI tools rather than ones provided by their employer
50%
of US workers don't know whether AI is allowed at their job
21%
feel confident using AI at work, lower than the 28% confident using it in everyday life

The limiting factor isn't tools. It's permission, training, and judgement.

Sources: Microsoft Work Trend Index 2025 (shadow AI); KPMG Trust, attitudes and use of AI study 2025 (workplace guidance); UK Government AI Skills for Life and Work survey 2025 (workplace confidence).
